The proof claim ladder

A Crouzeix manuscript, a Lean source tree, and a passing Lean build make different claims.

  1. Source claim: the authors propose a proof.
  2. Formal target: a precise Lean proposition elaborates under pinned definitions.
  3. Build claim: pinned source compiles in a fresh, hermetic environment.
  4. Trust claim: the audit records which axioms and imported declarations the theorem uses.

A receipt binds the command, inputs, logs, and audit. It does not prove the prose-to-Lean correspondence by itself; that is why the source map exists.
