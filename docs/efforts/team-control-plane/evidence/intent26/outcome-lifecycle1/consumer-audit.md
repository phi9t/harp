Research verifier admission is a narrow additional route through existing Episode/workspace APIs. The exact currently admitted investigation_complete contract pins verifier, intent, acceptance, policy, suite and environment into the immutable captured input and workspace manifest. Both mailbox admission and workspace creation revalidate those pins, role, current Attempt worker independence and existing epoch/generation checks. Start revalidates the manifest against the current research contract. Production and standalone no-candidate verifier paths retain their original candidate and binding rules. Original nullable Attempt provenance is preserved.

The seven assessment cases are represented by: (1) lifecycle_research_verifier_uses_exact_contract_without_production_candidate plus terminal/cached/Owner-target tests; (2) measured production amendment/latest-failed-gate tests; (3) unmapped_legacy_acceptance and historical_v1_mapping_then_explicit_owner_amendment tests; (4) delegated_source_amendment, prerequisite supersession, immutable prior Plan ceiling, and research source-input/prepared-execution tests; (5) unresolved_original_attempt_effect, amended production retirement and nullable lead provenance tests; (6) concurrent acceptance/input and amendment/old-source requests, changed-payload receipt conflicts, idempotent history checks, existing assignment/capacity and physical release/start races, and malformed migration refusal; (7) research wait/current projection and explicit dependent revalidation. Concurrent scheduling observes a permissible serialization; it does not claim every possible interleaving was forced.

Critique guardrails are covered in rows5/6/13 (exact acceptance exception), rows1/8 (amendment eligibility distinct from dispatch), rows6/7 (actual Owner target and raw evidence storage), and rows9/14/16 (current Owner cleanup with unknown usage retained).
