## What to build

Reconcile the I1 code, record and journal branches and publish an evidence matrix that assigns each selected mechanism `promote`, `re-prove`, `reject` or `I1-only` without merging or rewriting I1.

## Acceptance
- [ ] The harvest binds exact current I1 branch tips and records stale prose, commit history and ticket state as separate evidence sources.
- [ ] Every selected mechanism names the I2 requirement served, source paths/commit, state authority, failure model, tests, reviews, live episodes and gaps.
- [ ] Promotion decisions satisfy the risk-tiered proof floor for deterministic logic, provider/runtime behavior or authority/external effects.
- [ ] Approved promotions become focused I2 ticket proposals and cannot introduce a second execution-state ledger.
- [ ] No I1 ref, worktree, provider session or historical record is moved, resumed, rewritten or landed wholesale.
