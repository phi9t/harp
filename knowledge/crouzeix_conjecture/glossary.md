# Crouzeix conjecture glossary

> This file is a maintained Harp technical packet. It separates Harp's
> synthesis from primary-source claims; primary-source receipts and local
> verification logs live under `evidence/`.

## Numerical range

For an operator $A$ on a complex Hilbert space,

$$
W(A)=\{\langle Ax,x\rangle:\|x\|=1\}.
$$

In finite dimension it is compact and convex. In infinite dimension its
closure is used for the spectral-set statement.

## Spectral set

A compact set $K$ containing the spectrum of $A$ is a $C$-spectral set
when

$$
\|r(A)\|\le C\sup_{z\in K}|r(z)|
$$

for rational $r$ with poles off $K$.

## Completely bounded spectral set

The matrix-amplified version asks for the same bound uniformly for
matrix-valued rational functions. Neither proof in this packet directly
supplies that strengthening.

## Double-layer map

The positive operator-valued boundary construction associated with a convex
domain containing $W(A)$. It combines a Cauchy layer and its adjoint. Both
proofs use the resulting identity, but they preserve different families of
functions after it.

## Companion transform

The adjoint-side term in a symmetrized identity such as

$$
2\Phi(f)=f(A)+\alpha(f)(A)^*.
$$

The constant-two problem is not solved by bounding the two summands
independently.

## Cayley family

For $|wf|<1$, the family

$$
c_w=\frac{1+wf}{1-wf}=1+2\sum_{m\ge1}w^m f^m.
$$

Jin pushes this whole family through the positive double-layer map.

## Positive-real function

A matrix-valued analytic function $H$ on $\mathbb D$ with
$\operatorname{Re}H(w)\succeq0$. Its matrix Herglotz kernel is positive.

## Positive-real completion

Jin's finite-dimensional interface: a positive-real $H$ completes
$(I-wT)^{-1}$ modulo the adjoint algebra generated by an auxiliary
simple-spectrum matrix $B$. The target eigenvalues may repeat.

## Herglotz kernel

For a positive-real matrix function $H$,

$$
\mathcal L_H(w,z)=\frac{H(w)+H(z)^*}{1-w\overline z}.
$$

Positivity means every finite sampled block matrix has a nonnegative quadratic
form, including lists with repeated sample points.

## Simple spectrum

An auxiliary matrix has simple spectrum when its eigenvalues are distinct.
This makes its generated algebra diagonal in an eigenbasis. It does not require
the target values $f(\beta_i)$ to be distinct.

## Weighted Gramian

A norm-convergent series such as

$$
\sum_{k\ge0}a^{-k}T^{*k}T^k.
$$

Jin compares the versions with $a=4$ and $a=2$.

## 2-dilation

In the Lorist-Schwenninger lemma, the power relation

$$
T^{*n}=2V^*Q^{*n}V-E_n
$$

expresses the powers of $T^*$ as a compressed contraction-power family plus
a uniformly bounded perturbation.

## Perturbation family

The commuting operators $E_n$ in the 2-dilation relation. Their complete
power-indexed behavior drives the scalar recurrence.

## Uniform algebra

A closed unital subalgebra of continuous functions on a compact space, with
the supremum norm. Lorist-Schwenninger state an abstract constant-two variant
for a bounded homomorphism paired with a unital bounded antilinear map.
