route / selected prerequisite coverage” identifies concepts treated by this
packet; it never claims complete coverage of a source chapter. “Advanced
follow-on” names where the remaining chapter material belongs. See the

## Advanced tracks

The foundation route deliberately stops where machinery begins to dominate
modelling intuition. The next releases are: neural networks; kernels and sparse kernel
machines; graphical models; mixture and latent-variable models; approximate
inference; sampling; sequential models; ensembles and model combination;
matrix factorizations and canonical forms; tensor methods; convexity and
duality; linear dynamical systems; functional analysis; Perron-Frobenius
theory; and advanced iterative methods.

The prerequisite spine is modules 1–2 for numerical tracks, modules 3–4 for
probabilistic tracks, and modules 5–6 for optimisation-heavy tracks. It is a
preparation route rather than a replacement for any complete source chapter.
