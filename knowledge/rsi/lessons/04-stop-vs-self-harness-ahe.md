# STOP versus Self-Harness and AHE

## Observe

STOP optimizes an improver against an external utility. Self-Harness adds
held-in and held-out acceptance for harness edits. AHE adds trace-grounded
diagnosis and observability constraints.

## Predict

Separate recursive syntax inside an improver from external acceptance,
evaluator integrity, and measurement of later improvement production.

## Compare

All three remain `harness-improvement` in this Atlas. STOP recursively invokes
an improver, but fixed utility and fixed model scope do not establish full RSI.
Self-Harness and AHE strengthen acceptance and attribution without measuring
next-cycle gain.

## Explain

[[knowledge/rsi/chapters/evaluation-promotion-containment|Evaluation, promotion, and containment]]
shows why proposer recursion, acceptance, and successor measurement are
separate contracts.

## Missing fact

next accepted harness changes under the same proposer, tasks, permissions, and
