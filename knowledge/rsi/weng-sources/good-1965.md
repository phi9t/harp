# Ultraintelligence as a Successor-Machine Loop

## Problem

Good asks what machine could exceed human intellectual activity. Section 1, captured at lines 23–32 and 62–68, ties its design to understanding thought or the brain and calls the work early-stage speculation.

## Core mechanism

Section 2, captured at lines 106–122, defines an ultraintelligent machine as surpassing every human intellectual activity. Since machine design is one such activity, it could design better successors or improve itself, producing an intelligence explosion.

## Reported evidence

This is a conceptual argument, not an experiment. Captured lines 62–68 and 95–103 propose successive approximation through speculation, theory, and experiment, but report no blueprint or measurement.

## Key limitation

Captured lines 95–103 explicitly rule out a hardware blueprint. Lines 113–115 condition the loop’s value on control, but provide no control or validation mechanism.

## Why Weng cites it

Weng’s introduction, captured at evidence/weng/text/weng-harness.txt lines 24–30, uses Good for the historical intelligence-explosion frame, then separates that lineage from harness engineering. It is not evidence for a modern harness.
