# Verified coevolution agenda claim evidence ledger

Mode: `CLAIM LEDGER`.

Each material claim in the agenda should link to one stable `VCA-*` heading.
Local locators are evidence ceilings, not permission to infer broader results.

## VCA-001 Recursive Closure Definition

**Claim.** Harp defines recursive self-improvement as recursive closure over an
improvement process: accepted change must improve a future proposer, evaluator,
experience representation, search policy, editable scope, or promotion rule.

**Evidence.** Harp synthesis in
`knowledge/verified_coevolution_agenda/verified_coevolution_agenda.md`;
theoretical precedent from `GODEL-MACHINE`; design input preserved at
`evidence/verified_coevolution_agenda/artifacts/supplied_research_agenda.txt`.

**Status.** Harp-authored definition.

**Boundary.** This is a classification rule for Harp. It does not claim that
all cited papers use this definition.

## VCA-002 Classification Levels

**Claim.** Levels 0 through 6 classify persistent update depth and later
consumer, but they are not a maturity ladder.

**Evidence.** Harp synthesis in the main report; existing Harp retained
concepts for recursive-improvement boundaries and evaluation containment.

**Status.** Harp-authored classification.

**Boundary.** A high level is not automatically safer, more useful, or more
scientifically mature than a lower level.

## VCA-003 Three-Axis Field Map

**Claim.** Systems should be compared across editable substrate, update
mechanism, and acceptance evidence.

**Evidence.** Existing Harp source rows for STOP, Meta-Harness, AHE, ADAS, DGM;
new agenda rows for LADDER, PRIME-TTRL, NSRSA, SAHOO, MODEL-COLLAPSE,
SCRIVENS-VERIFICATION, GODEL-AGENT, and MENDEL-GODEL-MACHINE.

**Status.** Harp-authored synthesis over source-backed local artifacts.

**Boundary.** Cross-paper placement is an analytical map, not evidence of direct
lineage unless an evidence-graph edge records a direct source-supported
relationship.

## VCA-006 TTRL Distinction

**Claim.** LADDER-TTRL and PRIME-RL TTRL are distinct mechanisms and should not
be collapsed into one TTRL row.

**Evidence.** `LADDER` full-text capture and `PRIME-TTRL` full-text capture.

**Status.** Harp-authored comparison.

**Boundary.** The comparison is mechanism-level. It does not rank the methods
or assert a shared benchmark scale.

## VCA-007 Coevolution Hypothesis

**Claim.** Model-harness coevolution is a research hypothesis: controlled
updates across weights, harness structure, experience, and verification may
produce gains that single-surface adaptation misses.

**Evidence.** Existing Harp rows for Meta-Harness, AHE, DGM, ADAS, and SIA;
new agenda synthesis in the main report.

**Status.** Hypothesis.

**Boundary.** Do not describe coevolution as an established historical law.
Existing systems usually optimize one surface at a time or leave crucial
authority fixed.

## VCA-008 Search-to-Skill Hypothesis

**Claim.** Successful search traces, reusable skills, and failure-recovery
pairs may be internalized into model weights and then free the harness to search
at a more abstract level.

**Evidence.** Harp synthesis from existing harness-search packets and the
supplied research agenda.

**Status.** Hypothesis.

**Boundary.** Requires explicit experiments. A successful trace archive alone
does not prove model internalization or transfer.

## VCA-009 NSRSA Boundary

**Claim.** NSRSA is evidence for verifier-gated reasoning-data quality control
in recursive self-training, not evidence of broad alignment.

**Evidence.** `NSRSA`, especially
`evidence/verified_coevolution_agenda/artifacts/nsrsa/text/arxiv-2603.21558v1.raw.txt`.

**Status.** Source-backed mechanism plus Harp boundary.

**Boundary.** Does not establish corrigibility, honesty, non-deception,
tool-use policy, distributional safety, or long-horizon goal stability.

## VCA-010 SAHOO Boundary

**Claim.** SAHOO is empirical monitoring and acceptance control through drift
signals, constraints, and regression-risk checks; it is not proof that goals
remain stable.

**Evidence.** `SAHOO`, especially
`evidence/verified_coevolution_agenda/artifacts/sahoo/text/arxiv-2603.06333v1.raw.txt`.

**Status.** Source-backed mechanism plus Harp boundary.

**Boundary.** The agenda must still ask what happens when the improving system
learns to model or manipulate the monitor.

## VCA-011 Drift and Regression Boundaries

**Claim.** Recursive failure modes should separate epistemic drift,
distribution collapse, capability regression, objective drift, evaluator drift,
and search collapse.

**Evidence.** `MODEL-COLLAPSE` for recursive generated-data collapse; `NSRSA`
for reasoning-trace filtering; `SAHOO` for goal-drift monitoring; existing
Harp archive and evaluator-integrity packets.

**Status.** Harp-authored failure taxonomy.

**Boundary.** A mitigation for one failure mode does not solve the others.

## VCA-012 Model-Collapse Boundary

**Claim.** Recursive training on generated data can progressively lose support
from the original data distribution.

**Evidence.** `MODEL-COLLAPSE`,
`evidence/verified_coevolution_agenda/artifacts/model_collapse/text/nature-s41586-024-07566-y.txt`.

**Status.** Source-backed article claim.

**Boundary.** The Nature article is a risk anchor. It is not a complete theory
of all recursive self-improvement failure modes.

## VCA-013 Scrivens Boundary

**Claim.** Scrivens is conditional theory about statistical gates and property
verification in a sequential self-improvement model.

**Evidence.** `SCRIVENS-VERIFICATION`, especially
`evidence/verified_coevolution_agenda/artifacts/scrivens_verification/text/arxiv-2603.28650v1.raw.txt`.

**Status.** Source-backed theoretical claim plus Harp boundary.

**Boundary.** Certificates apply to encoded properties over explicit domains.
Zero false acceptance for a modeled gate is not semantic alignment.
