# Harp knowledge home

Open the Harp repository root as the Obsidian vault. This keeps the
maintained explanations in `knowledge/`, their captured source material in
`evidence/`, and the supporting contracts visible without copied files or
symlinked vault roots.

Use the [[knowledge/obsidian/harp_knowledge.base|Knowledge Base]] to browse
metadata-backed reader routes, or open the
[[knowledge/obsidian/harp_knowledge_map.canvas|Knowledge Canvas]] for a small
navigational map.

## Start with a question

- **How should I orient myself to recursive self-improvement?** Start at the
  [[knowledge/rsi/rsi_index|RSI orientation index]].
- **Which systems make different improvement-loop choices?** Compare the
  [[knowledge/rsi/systems/system_readings_index|system readings]] before
  treating a shared label as a shared mechanism.
- **What supports a claim?** Inspect the
  [[knowledge/rsi/claim_evidence_ledger|RSI claim ledger]], the
  [[knowledge/rsi/source_registry|source registry]], and the
  [[knowledge/rsi/missing_evidence|missing-evidence ledger]].
- **How do I learn the evaluator and systems foundations?** Follow the
  [[knowledge/rsi/sicp/sicp_index|SICP systems and evaluator course]].
- **Which benchmarks can establish what?** Read the
  [[knowledge/harness_benchmarks/harness_benchmark_field_guide|harness benchmark field guide]].
- **How does a population search retain harness gains?** Inspect the
  [[knowledge/darwinx/darwinx_index|DarwinX packet]].
- **How do the two Crouzeix proof routes differ?** Explore the
  [[knowledge/crouzeix_conjecture/crouzeix_conjecture_index|Crouzeix conjecture packet]].

> [!info] Evidence-aware reading
> A claim ledger assigns a claim class and its precise supporting route. A
> source registry identifies the primary source and capture boundary. A
> missing-evidence ledger states what Harp does not possess or establish.
> Follow all three before promoting a useful route or graph connection into an
> evidence claim.

`knowledge/` is Harp's technical-prose authority. `evidence/` is captured
source material. Membership in a graph, Base view, or Canvas map is navigation
only; it is not evidence. For the corresponding authoring boundary, see
[[docs/writing-style/STYLE_GUIDE|credible documentation style]].
