Source cards

SIA's Feedback-Agent selects either a harness update or a weight update at a step; across the overall SIA-W+H run, generation-specific or selected-best composites can contain both changed harness and changed LoRA weights. Reported gains retain model-choice and baseline confounds.

CONTINUAL-HARNESS refines prompt, subagents, skills, and memory without resetting the environment. Low-reward windows are scored, relabeled by a stronger teacher, and used for soft policy updates. The teacher, process reward, and game evaluator remain external.

Compare cells and confounds

Each update changes the other's data distribution. A stronger teacher may supply the decisive capability. A learned policy may exploit evaluator leakage. A new harness may help old weights but interfere with new weights. Goodhart pressure can raise the measured reward without improving the intended behavior.
