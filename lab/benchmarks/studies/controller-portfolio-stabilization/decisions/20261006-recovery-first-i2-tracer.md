slice after stabilization and I1 evidence harvest. It admits one exact WorkRef,
IntentRevision and AcceptanceContract; creates one fenced Attempt and isolated
worktree inside the developer Insula; launches one GPT-5.5 TraeCLI worker through

that no worker, effect or authority is duplicated. Completion produces a
human-approved landing Action Packet and never auto-merges.

and the bounded I1 harvest is approved. The current DevX/Insula implementation
