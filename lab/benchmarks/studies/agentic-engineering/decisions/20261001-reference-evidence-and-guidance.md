# Decision: reference evidence, guidance and initial budgets

Date: 2026-10-01.
Authority: the owner answered "accept" to recommendations Q7–Q9.

## Accepted decisions

1. A fresh clone retains decisions, acceptance results, exact commits,
   execution contracts, artifact digests, limitations and superseding
   corrections. Raw logs remain local under explicit retention and redaction
   rules. Claims whose supporting records are unavailable are labeled.
2. Provide one entry point to a shared workflow, focused reference pages and
   small Codex/Claude adapters. Current instructions and historical experiments
   are distinct. Each maintained practice states its trigger, action,
   completion criterion and evidence.
3. Initial reference runs use at most two concurrent workers, a 30-minute cap
   per worker and at most three repair rounds. Record limits before launch;
   increasing them requires explicit authorization. Capture token usage when
   available and mark missing cost data.

## Limits

The launch budget is a design default, not authorization to spend on live runs
in this session. Total run-count limits, raw-log retention duration, concrete
adapter behavior and rollout sequence are proposed in the design briefing
and still need owner confirmation. These defaults have not been written into
the synced policy or implemented in the toolbox.
