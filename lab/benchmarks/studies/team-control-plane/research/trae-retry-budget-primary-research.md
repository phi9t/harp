## Independent enforceable boundary

Request admission and provider forwarding are different counters. A trusted sole-egress proxy must atomically reserve **each incoming attempt**, including retry, discovery, denied request and continuation, before forwarding; independently count forwarded provider calls, reserved input/output tokens and worst-case price. Reject absent/oversized generation limits before upstream I/O, enforce allowlisted route/model and content/body caps, and reserve worst-case per-request spend against the aggregate ceiling before forwarding. Verify provider honors the generation cap or require a proven provider hard quota; merely stopping a local stream cannot prove upstream generation/billing stopped.

For the synthetic six-attempt envelope, on attempt six reaching its terminal outcome, fail closed and stop the independently supervised whole runtime namespace before another dispatch. Merely returning 429 at attempt seven is already a seventh attempted request and may provoke more. A server can prevent **forwarded** call seven by refusing it, but cannot guarantee the client never attempts call seven without proven client retry/continuation suppression and dispatch ownership. State which contract is proven; never label six forwarded as six attempted. Synthetic-only execution has zero external provider spend, but a byte/response length cap alone is not a token bound for future paid models.
